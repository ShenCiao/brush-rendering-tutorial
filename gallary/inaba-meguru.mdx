### About {#about}

ShenCiao, the developer of this website, spent over 100 hours creating this illustration in [Ciallo](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3973810), the vector painting software he developed. It is the first illustration made with Ciallo and the world's first commercial-quality bishōjo vector illustration.

The character is Inaba Meguru from Yuzusoft's _Sabbat of the Witch_. The developer worked to recreate the [official illustration](https://www.suruga-ya.jp/product/detail/120076688).

You can also view the [line art of this illustration](./inaba-meguru-lineart.mdx).

This is fan art of a Yuzusoft work. Commercial use is strictly prohibited.
